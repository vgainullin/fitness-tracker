Iron Log

A fast, free workout tracker for the gym and for home training.

Open Iron Log

What it does

  • Log sets, reps and weight as you train, with last session's numbers filled in.
  • Pick exercises quickly, with suggestions for muscles you haven't trained lately.
  • Train at home with whatever equipment you have.
  • Track muscle progress and body weight over time.
  • Optionally chat with an AI coach using your own Anthropic API key.

Your data stays yours

You sign in with Google. Iron Log creates one spreadsheet, Iron Log Data, in your own Google Drive and saves your workouts there. It asks only for access to files it creates, so it can't see anything else in your Drive.

There is no Iron Log server or database. Nobody else, including the developer, can see your workouts. Details are in the privacy policy.
