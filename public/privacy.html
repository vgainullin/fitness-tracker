Privacy policy

Last updated 4 October 2026

Summary

Iron Log has no server and no database. Your workout data is stored in your own Google Drive and on your own device. The developer never receives it.

Google account data

When you sign in with Google, Iron Log asks for:

  • Your email address and account ID, used only on your device to keep your data separate from anyone else who uses the same browser.
  • Access to files Iron Log creates in your Drive (the drive.file permission). Iron Log uses it to create and update one spreadsheet, Iron Log Data, and backup copies you ask for. It cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.

Requests go straight from your browser to Google. Iron Log does not sell Google user data, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI models. Workout data from your spreadsheet leaves your browser only when you use the optional AI coach, as described below. Its use of data from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data on your device

Settings, presets, home equipment, your in-progress workout, and workouts waiting to sync are kept in your browser's storage, separately for each Google account. Signing out leaves them on the device for that account only; clearing your browser's site data removes them.

AI coach (optional)

If you add your own Anthropic API key, it is stored on your device. When you use the coach, your question and the workout data it needs are sent to Anthropic through a relay that passes them on without storing them. Anthropic's own privacy policy applies to that data. If you don't add a key, nothing is sent.

Deleting your data

Delete the Iron Log Data spreadsheet from your Google Drive, clear this site's data in your browser, and remove Iron Log's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/vgainullin/fitness-tracker.
